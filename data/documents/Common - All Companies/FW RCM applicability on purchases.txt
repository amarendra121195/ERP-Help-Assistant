Dear all,

Many queries are coming on reverse charge (RCM), so sharing a quick summary again. Please keep this mail for reference.

Under RCM the BUYER (we) pays GST to the government, not the vendor. The vendor invoice will not charge GST in these cases.

Common RCM cases for our group companies:
 - Legal services from an advocate or law firm
 - Goods Transport Agency (GTA) services, where the GTA has not opted to pay tax under forward charge
 - Security services from a non-corporate supplier (individual / partnership firm) to a registered company

In D365 please select the item sales tax group marked "RCM" on such PO lines / invoices. The system will then post both the GST liability and the input credit. Do not use the normal GST group for these.

If you are not sure whether RCM applies, please write to the tax team BEFORE posting the invoice. Correction after GST return filing is difficult.
