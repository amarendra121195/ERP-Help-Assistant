GRN / Product receipt posting - common errors (IT helpdesk notes)
Maintained by: Nikhil (IT helpdesk)   last update 02-Sep-2026
------------------------------------------------------------------

1) "Purchase order is not confirmed"
   -> PO must be approved AND confirmed before we can receive. Ask buyer to confirm it. Store cannot do anything here.

2) "Batch number is mandatory" / batch error
   -> item is batch tracked. Open Registration on the line, enter batch no. then post.
   Note: GRN post karne se pehle batch number zaroor daalo, warna stock wrong batch mein chala jayega.

3) "Quantity exceeds over-delivery tolerance"
   -> recieved qty is more than PO qty + allowed tolerance (normally 5%).
   Receive only PO qty, extra qty send back or ask buyer to amend PO.

4) "Financial dimension Cost centre is missing"
   -> PO line has no cost centre. Buyer has to update financial dimensions on PO line. After that GRN will post.

5) Receipt posted in wrong warehouse
   -> do NOT cancel yourself. raise ticket, IT/stores team will do the correction with a transfer journal.

For anything not in this list -> helpdesk ticket with PO no + screenshot.
